Ada Lovelace

The Enchantress of Numbers and the first computer programmer.

Highlights of Ada's Life:

  • 1815 - Born in London to the poet Lord Byron and Anne Isabella Milbanke.
  • 1833 - Met Charles Babbage, the inventor of the Difference Engine.
  • 1843 - Published her notes on Babbage's Analytical Engine, containing what is now considered the first computer algorithm.
  • Vision - She was the first to realize that computers could do more than just pure math; they could create music or art if programmed correctly.

"That brain of mine is something more than merely mortal; as time will show."

-- Ada Lovelace
